RELIA NeuroIntegration Coaching

RELIA coaching is a neurointegrative change process. It combines attuned relationship, body-based experiences, and reflective integration to help the nervous system feel safer, more organized, and more connected in everyday life — strengthening the vagus–body–brain connection and growing interoception: the ability to notice and respond to what is happening inside.

R

Relational

We start with a real, safe-enough relationship — consistent, non-shaming, and attuned — where your nervous system can begin to relax and feel seen.

"Without relational safety, the nervous system will not risk new experiences."
E

Embodied

We work through the body, not just the head. Sessions use movement, posture, breath, sensory input, and play to create new, regulated body states — strengthening interoception in an intentional, relational context.

L

Listening

We learn to listen inward and outward — noticing what the body says inside (tension, energy, breath, gut feelings) and what relationships say outside (tone, pacing, cues).

"Instead of drowning in sensations and emotions, you start to understand them and respond more flexibly."
I

Identity

We honor who you are, not just what you do — rewriting old nervous system narratives ("I'm too much," "I can't cope") in a more compassionate, neurobiologically accurate way.

A

Attunement & Integration

We weave it into daily life — practicing attuned responses within yourself and between you and others, so new nervous system patterns are reinforced in real moments: at home, at work, and in relationships.

"RELIA coaching turns relational, embodied, moment-to-moment experiences into lasting changes in how the nervous system reads the world, responds to stress, and experiences self and others."

Neuro-auditory support — Tomatis® listening & bone conduction

As part of RELIA coaching, some clients benefit from a neuro-auditory program called Tomatis®. This method uses carefully filtered music and sound delivered through both the ears and bone conduction to stimulate the ear–brain connection — improving how the nervous system processes sound, balance, and body cues, which directly supports interoception and regulation.

For nervous systems that are highly sensitive, easily overloaded by sound, or disconnected from internal cues, Tomatis® listening provides an additional, gentle way to reorganize sensory input so the body can feel safer and more settled from the inside out.

Who this is for

Adults living with chronic stress or dysregulation, parents who want to co-regulate better, and helping professionals seeking a deeper, neuroscience-grounded framework. Offered hybrid — in person in California and online nationwide.

About

Sanette Louwrens, OTR/L

Sanette is an occupational therapist and neurointegrative coach who helps adults move from chronic survival mode toward a more regulated, connected way of living. RELIA coaching connects relational safety, embodied experience, and reflective integration — because the nervous system never works in pieces.

  • Tomatis® Level 4 Consultant — neuro-auditory listening & bone conduction
